What is Habitus?

Habitus is your personal habit tracking companion. Build streaks, unlock achievements, and celebrate your progress with seasonal themes that keep things fresh and exciting.

  • 📊 Track Daily Habits - Monitor your progress with beautiful visualizations
  • 🔥 Build Streaks - Keep your momentum going day after day
  • 🏆 Unlock Achievements - Earn badges for your milestones
  • 🎨 Seasonal Themes - Enjoy dynamic themes throughout the year
